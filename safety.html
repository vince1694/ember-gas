Safety First

Your safety is our top priority. Follow these simple tips to keep your household safe.

Ventilation

Keep your kitchen well-ventilated while cooking to prevent gas accumulation.

Leak Check

Regularly apply soapy water to the hose and regulator to check for bubbles. If you see bubbles, there's a leak.

Certified Parts

Only use high-quality, certified regulators and high-pressure hoses. Replace them every 2 years or as recommended.
